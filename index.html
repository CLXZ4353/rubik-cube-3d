Controls

Turns

U D L R F B — turn the face in your point of view (they follow the camera — no y needed)

Shift + key — counter-clockwise

Hold 2 + key — double turn

M E S — middle slices (absolute)

x y z — rotate the whole cube (absolute)

The centre cubie of the face you are looking at blinks.

Actions

Ctrl/⌘ Z — undo

Ctrl/⌘ Y — redo

Esc — reset to solved

H — toggle this help

Start — timer mode: count down from the set time

Stop — freeze the timer (only while it runs)

− 5s / + 5s — change the time in the timer

Mouse / Touch

Drag — orbit the cube

Scroll / pinch — zoom

Press H or tap ? to close.
